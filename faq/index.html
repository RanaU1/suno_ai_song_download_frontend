A free tool that downloads public Suno songs from their share link as MP3, M4A or WAV. It can also split a song into instrumental and vocals, and cut a short preview sample. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Suno.

Open the song on Suno, copy its share link, paste it into the box on the home page, choose a format and press Download Song. The full steps are on the How it works page.

Public Suno share links, for example https://suno.com/s/XXXXXXXXXXXX. The song has to be public: private songs and links that need a login cannot be downloaded. If a link is rejected, open the song on Suno and use Share → Copy link to get a fresh one.

MP3 plays everywhere. M4A is the default: small files with very good quality, great for phones. WAV is uncompressed and very large, useful if you plan to edit the audio. WAV is made from the same source audio, so it is bigger, not better: it cannot add detail that was never there.

The website is free and needs no account, no sign-in and no email address.

Only for a few minutes. Songs, covers, stems and samples are created for you on request and are removed from the server automatically, usually within about five minutes. Save your file right after it appears. Details are in the Privacy policy.

It splits the song into an instrumental and a vocals-only file using audio signal processing. It is a quick approximation, not a studio-grade result: you may still hear a little vocal in the instrumental, and the other way round.

It cuts a short preview clip from your song with a fade at the end. Pick a preset length (auto, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes or half the song) and press Generate sample.

Check that the song is public and the link is complete, then try again. If the server was busy the request may time out after about a minute. The coloured dot in the menu shows the server state, and the status page shows it in detail. If it stays red or the problem continues, contact us.

This website gives you no rights to any song. What you may do with a Suno song depends on Suno’s terms and on the plan the song was created under. Please read Suno’s own terms before you publish or sell anything. See also the Disclaimer.

No accounts, no ads and no analytics trackers. We only handle the technical data needed to run the service and to deal with abuse, such as someone sending a very large number of requests. The Privacy policy explains exactly what is and is not collected.
