Reporting a problem? Please include

  • The Suno link you used (public links only).
  • The format you chose (MP3, M4A or WAV).

  • Your device and browser, for example “Android, Chrome”.

Copyright and removal requests

If you are the rights holder of a song and want something removed, write to us with the link to the song and a short explanation of your claim. Files created on this site are deleted automatically within minutes and nothing is published or shared, but we will review your message and act where appropriate.

Response time

This is a small project run by one person. We read every message but cannot promise an exact reply time. Before writing, the FAQ and the status page often answer the question straight away.
