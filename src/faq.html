🎵 A free tool that downloads public Suno songs from their share link as MP3, M4A or WAV. It can also split a song into instrumental and vocals ✂️ and cut a short preview sample.

It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Suno.

📋 Open the song on Suno, copy its share link and paste it into the box on the home page.

⬇️ Choose a format and press Download Song. The full steps are on the How it works page.

🔗 Public Suno share links, for example https://suno.com/s/XXXXXXXXXXXX.

The song has to be public: private songs and links that need a login cannot be downloaded. If a link is rejected, open the song on Suno and use Share → Copy link to get a fresh one.

🎧 MP3 plays everywhere. M4A is the default: small files with very good quality, great for phones. WAV is uncompressed and very large, useful if you plan to edit the audio.

WAV is made from the same source audio, so it is bigger, not better: it cannot add detail that was never there.
