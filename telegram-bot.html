Telegram bot

Get your Suno songs right inside Telegram

Send a public Suno link to the bot and it replies with the audio file. No browser needed.

Open the Telegram bot
1

Open the bot

Tap the button above and press Start in Telegram.

2

Send a link

Paste a public Suno song link as a normal message.

3

Get the audio

The bot shows the song details and sends the audio file to your chat.

Good to know

  • It works with public Suno links only.
  • Telegram limits the size of files a bot can send, so a very long song may not arrive. In that case use the website, which can also give you the song as WAV.
  • Using the bot means using Telegram, which has its own privacy policy.
  • The bot is independent and not affiliated with Suno. See the Terms and Privacy policy.
