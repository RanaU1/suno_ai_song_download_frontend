How it works

Download a Suno song in four steps

No account, no installation. Works in any modern browser on phone or computer.

1

Copy the link

Open the song on Suno, press Share and copy the link. The song must be public.

2

Paste it

Paste the link into the box on the home page. On a phone, use the Paste button.

3

Pick a format

Choose MP3, M4A or WAV. Not sure? M4A is a good default.

4

Press Download

Wait a few seconds, listen to the preview, then press Save Full Song.

Which format is right for me?

Sizes are rough figures for a typical song.

FormatBest forSize
MP3Maximum compatibility: cars, old players, any appabout 2.4 MB per minute at 320 kbps
M4APhones and everyday listening, small filesabout the same as MP3 or smaller
WAVEditing in a music or video programabout 10 MB per minute

WAV is uncompressed, but it is made from the same source audio. It is larger, not better.

Track and vocals

After a song loads, split it into an instrumental and a vocals-only file. It uses audio signal processing, so treat the result as a quick approximation.

Preview sample

Cut a short clip with a fade-out: auto, 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes or half of the song.

Save your files right away. Everything created for you is deleted from the server automatically after a few minutes.
