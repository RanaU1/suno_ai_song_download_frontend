About

A small tool built to be simple and fast

One job done well: turn a public Suno link into an audio file you can keep.

What this site is

Suno Ai Song Download lets you download public Suno songs as MP3, M4A or WAV, split a song into instrumental and vocals, and create short preview samples. It is made and maintained by RanaUniverse as an independent project.

Why it exists

Creators often want a quick, clean copy of their own songs, for a video, a demo or an offline playlist, without installing software or creating yet another account. This site tries to make that a ten-second job.

What it is not

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Suno. We do not host a music library and we do not publish anyone’s songs. Everything is created on request from a link you provide and removed again within minutes. See the Disclaimer.

No accounts

Paste a link and go. No sign-up, no email.

No tracking

No ads and no analytics trackers on this site.

Short-lived files

Files are deleted automatically within minutes.

Phone friendly

Designed to work as well on a phone as on a PC.

How it is built

The website you are looking at is a static site. A small Python service on our own server fetches the song, converts it with FFmpeg and sends you the file. A companion Telegram bot does the same thing inside Telegram.
